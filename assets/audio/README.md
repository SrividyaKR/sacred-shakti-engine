# Audio beds

Put a licensed track per deity at `assets/audio/<id>/<id>_theme_15s.mp3` (15 seconds, with a 0.5 s fade-in and 1.5 s fade-out so it loops). `scripts/run_pipeline.py` muxes it into the rendered video automatically.

Only use audio you have the right to publish: your own, CC0 / Pixabay-licensed, YouTube Audio Library, or audio you have written permission for. A video being free to watch on YouTube does not make it free to reuse. Audio files here are git-ignored so third-party tracks are never redistributed in the repo.

Alternative with no licensing issue: Seedance 2.5 can generate synchronized audio itself (`--audio`); OpenRouter lists the same price with or without audio.
